Frequently Asked Questions

The practical details about timing, revisions, privacy, and sharing your finished EchoStory.

How long does an EchoStory take?

Most packages are delivered in 7–14 days after we confirm your details. Larger packages (Signature+) may take 14–21 days. Rush options are available for last-minute celebrations—just mention it in your request.

Do I get to request changes?

Yes. We include one revision round with every package. After you receive your EchoStory, you can request adjustments to lyrics, music style, or artwork. We want it to feel right for your person.

Will my stories and information be kept private?

Yes. The information you share is treated as confidential. Your EchoStory is private and accessible through the link you receive. We do not publish your stories, photos, or personal details as part of the public site.

Can I gift this if the person isn't tech-savvy?

Yes. Every EchoStory includes a QR code that works on a smartphone—point the camera and scan. We also provide printable keepsakes and PDFs that can be used offline. The experience is designed to stay simple for the recipient.

Head back to the guided experience and start with the vibe, occasion, and memories that make the person unmistakably them.
